# 5. eat-effects — eat a golden apple in mid-air, get its effects, no disconnect.
wait 22
send /gamemode creative
wait 2
send /inventory creativegive 36 GoldenApple 1
wait 2
send /changeslot 1
wait 1
send /tp 0 200 0
wait 1
send /look up
wait 1
send /useitem
wait 5
send /effects
wait 3
expect Regeneration|Absorption
reject kicked
reject Disconnected
reject Unsupported
